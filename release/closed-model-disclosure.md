## Why this exists

For closed models, you cannot inspect or document the weights, training
corpus, or internal behavior. What you CAN document is the exact endpoint,
model string, prompt, settings, and what came out - and an explicit list of
what remains unverifiable. That's not a weakness in your work; it's the
honest boundary of what's knowable from outside the provider.
